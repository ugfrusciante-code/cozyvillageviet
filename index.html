Village

Your village saves itself as you play, and picks up where you left off when you come back.

How to play

Camera

Drag with the left or right mouse button, or WASD, to pan · Q / E or middle-drag to rotate · scroll to zoom · click the minimap to jump.

Building

Pick from the bar at the bottom, then click the ground. R rotates. Hold Shift to keep placing. Esc or right-click cancels. Roads are drawn by dragging. Fields, pastures and orchards are drag-drawn: press, drag out the plot you want, release.

The loop

Gatherers stock their own huts. Porters and idle labourers carry goods onward. Market workers stock the stalls, and every household shops at the nearest stall. A home that receives enough food variety, fuel, clothing, water, faith, ale and beauty upgrades, houses more families, and pays more tax.

Farming

Fields are sown in spring, tended in summer, and reaped in autumn — an unreaped crop freezes in the field. Farming the same ground tires the soil (toggle the 🌱 overlay); rest it or move the field.

Economy tools

Select a workshop to cap its output or raise its priority. Select a building to see its supply lines. The Trading Post takes standing sell/buy orders.

Survival

Winter burns four times the firewood and stops the foragers. Get a hunter, a fishery or a farm running before the first snow, and build a granary so food stops spoiling.
